## The idea

Every Tuesday and Thursday the team account posts one player — her position, her numbers,
her best clip, and a tracked link to her page. We tag her own account so she can share it
to her network.

Right now a college coach only finds our girls if someone already pointed him there.
Nothing we do creates awareness with coaches who have never heard of this program. This
does.

---

## Why it's worth doing

- **Coaches see names before they meet them.** Familiarity is what makes a camp
  introduction — or a text from Joe — actually land.
- **Every girl gets a turn.** Not just the ones with the most film.
- **The program looks active.** When a coach does look us up, there's something current
  to find.
- **The players amplify it.** Tagging puts each post in front of her school, her
  teammates and her local coaches.
- **We can measure it.** Every link is tracked, so we'll see which posts brought coaches
  to a player's page.
