# X juice — strong guideline, not doctrine

Written 2026-08-18 after ranking 369 `@StarsNatWalker` originals (Jan 2024–Aug 2026) plus the girls’ own posts the team retweeted. Metricool only had two tweets ingested that day; the ranking is the public timeline.

This is a **strong default**. A true weekend, a real clip, or a number on her page beats the template. Do not invent a stat to fit it. Do not force every post into “championship voice.”

## What the numbers said

| Theme | Avg likes / original |
|---|---|
| Big-stage result (Scenic City, PGF, ACFL, a title game) | 54 |
| Team identity / roster (we are national, come watch, openings) | 54 |
| Named player **+ a number** (70 mph, bombs, a line) | 38 |
| Named player only (hometown, position, nice sentence) | 29 |
| No roster name | 27 |

Photo ~37 likes, video ~33, text-only ~15. Median original is 25 likes.

Naming a girl barely moves the needle. Putting her **on a named weekend** or **next to a measurable** does.

Retweet like counts belong to **her** tweet. Boosting her is right. It is not our juice.

## Team account (`@StarsNatWalker`)

**Job:** this club is national. A girl is proof, not the whole post.

Default shape:

1. Identity line: `Year · Position · City, State`
2. One sentence — lineup role + the travel tool (Joe’s language, not a bio)
3. Her number **or** her stage (PGF, Alliance All-Tournament, AFCS, a velo)
4. Shared team stage: Alliance 16U Tier III National Runner-Up
5. `@herhandle` + tracked player URL (shortener on)
6. Tag `@starsnationalfp` on the **image**, not in the caption
7. A still or a clip. No college tags. No hashtags.

**Lean this way**

- Same-day weekend post when we play: score, opponent, who showed up, one photo.
- Put a girl *inside* the stage. Kelsey-in-the-70-club worked because it was a club + video. Super 60 5–0 worked because the weekend was the post.
- Player-a-day is a recruiting file. Expect ~30 likes unless that day carries a stage or a number.

**Lean away**

- Hometown + position + “she’s a baller”
- Alliance runner-up as the *only* fact (it’s on every post; it becomes wallpaper)
- High school box scores on this account (travel / national / measurables)
- Counting a RT of Elise’s UVA camp as *our* 75 likes

If the page has no number and no travel honor except Alliance, **post it anyway** and mark the ask (velo, pop, film). Do not fabricate the missing piece.

## Each player (her account)

**Job:** I was there. I did this. Here is the number.

The posts that moved when we amplified them were first-person and specific: Elise at UVA (one inning, three punchouts), Sophia at Creighton (tour, live reps), Natalie at USA All-Americans, Lyla’s two-hour local workout, Baylee at UNC camp, Keira at MLB RBI. “Loved competing this summer, can’t wait for fall” was the miss.

**Lean this way**

- She posts first, same day. Place name + one fact + a photo or a short clip.
- She may tag the camp or school she just walked (max two college handles, and only a real thread).
- She tags `@StarsNatWalker` and `@starsnationalfp`. We RT.
- On her series day she quote-tweets the team post in her voice. No second clip if the team already used the money one.

**Lean away**

- Feelings with no place and no number
- Burning the team’s money clip on her account first (Ayn’s 8.86 / PGF is the standing example)
- Us writing a caption and asking her to copy-paste

A girl who won’t post still gets a team original. She just won’t get the 60–75 like camp tweets.

## Recut rule for drafts

When you open a queued tweet, ask only:

1. Is there a **stage** (named weekend or honor)?
2. Is there a **number** on her page?
3. Is there a **photo or clip**?

If two of three are yes, ship. If one, ship and write the ask. If none, it is a placeholder — get material or say so in the caption’s absence, not with fluff.
